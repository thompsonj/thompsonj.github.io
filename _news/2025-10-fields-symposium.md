I was invited to speak at the [Mini-Symposium on Cognition and Computation: Bidirectional Insights Between Cognitive Neuroscience and AI](https://www.fields.utoronto.ca/activities/25-26/neuroscience-cognition) at the Fields Institute for Research in Mathematical Sciences in Toronto. My talk was titled _AI as a theoretical framework for cognitive (neuro)science and cognitive (neuro)science to audit AI alignment_. A write-up of the talk appears in [_Fields Notes_, Volume 21:8, Summer 2026](https://www.fields.utoronto.ca/sites/default/files/FieldsNotes_Summer2026-digital.pdf).
