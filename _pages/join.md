From December 2026, I will be a Lecturer in the [School of Psychology and Neuroscience](https://www.gla.ac.uk/schools/psychologyneuroscience/) at the University of Glasgow, where I'm starting a research group. If you're curious about working together, please get in touch.

## Who I'm looking for

- **Students** looking for supervision for an undergraduate project, a master's project, or a PhD.
- **Postdocs** who would like to work with me, including through fellowships where I would be your host.
- **Collaborators** at any career stage, in or outside academia.
- **Visitors**: students or researchers who would like to spend time in the group, for example on a research visit or sabbatical.

## What I work on

My research has always crossed disciplinary boundaries. I have worked in cognitive science, computational neuroscience, deep learning, AI alignment, and philosophy of science, often several at once. The common thread is learning: how people learn, teach, communicate, and work together to solve problems, and how AI can both help us understand and support these capacities.

In Glasgow, a new focus will be collective learning: how groups learn, teach, and reason together, and what makes some communities wiser than their members and others less wise. You can read more about the questions I hope to pursue on my [research page]({{ '/research/#collective-learning-in-epistemic-communities' | relative_url }}).

I'm keen to hear from people working in any of the areas above, and I would also love to find collaborators in:

- metascience
- computational social science
- environmental psychology and the psychology of climate change

You don't need to fit neatly into any one of these. If your interests sit between fields, we may get along well.

## Funding

I don't currently have any open funded positions, but I'm happy to support applications for external funding, such as a PhD scholarship or a postdoctoral fellowship with me as your supervisor or host. Deadlines are often many months before the start date, so it's worth getting in touch early.

Schemes you could apply to include:

- **PhD:** [University of Glasgow scholarships](https://www.gla.ac.uk/scholarships/), ESRC studentships through the [Scottish Graduate School of Social Science](https://www.sgsss.ac.uk/studentships/), and funding from your home country for study abroad, such as [Commonwealth Scholarships](https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/), [Fulbright](https://fulbright.org.uk/), or the China Scholarship Council.
- **Postdocs:** [Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships), [Leverhulme Early Career Fellowships](https://www.leverhulme.ac.uk/early-career-fellowships), [British Academy Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://www.thebritishacademy.ac.uk/funding/postdoctoral-fellowships/), and, for researchers from outside the UK, [Royal Society Newton International Fellowships](https://royalsociety.org/grants/newton-international/).
- **Other:** foundations that fund research on AI and society sometimes offer scholarships and fellowships, though these change from year to year.

## How to get in touch

Email me at [jessica.thompson@psy.ox.ac.uk](mailto:jessica.thompson@psy.ox.ac.uk). It helps if your email includes:

- a little about who you are and what stage you're at
- what you'd like to work on, or which of my projects or papers caught your interest
- for students and postdocs: your CV, when you would hope to start, and whether you have funding or plan to apply for it
